Emergency Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Norton, MA

A ceiling stain means water is above it, and a sagging ceiling means it is holding a lot. Both need action before the drywall lets go.

A wet ceiling is an emergency when it sags, drips from a light fixture or grows by the hour. Emergency ceiling water damage calls in Norton go to our 24/7 line, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available.

Keep out from under any bulging ceiling. If it is safe to reach, poke a small hole to let water drain into a bucket, which relieves the weight. Turn off power to any fixture that is wet, and do not use it until it has been checked.

A sagging ceiling is an emergency

Drywall that holds water can fall without warning. A bulge that grows, a drip from a fixture or water running from a vent means the cavity above is full. Stay clear of the area and call.

Light fixtures are the main hazard. Water that enters a junction box can energize a metal fixture, so shut off the circuit at the panel if the panel is dry, and ask an electrician to check the wiring before the light is used again.

Roof leak or plumbing leak above the ceiling?

The source decides who fixes it. A stain under an upstairs bathroom points to a supply or drain line. A stain that appears after rain, near an exterior wall or under the attic points to the roof, a flashing or an ice dam.

A roofer fixes the roof, a plumber fixes the pipe, and the restoration crew handles the wet ceiling, insulation and framing. Our line takes a call before the source is known, and a crew can start by reading moisture and opening a small section to see what is above. How a wet ceiling is handled

The work moves from the top down, because water above a ceiling is the part you cannot see.

1

Relieve and protect

Bulges are drained and the floor below is covered. Contents are moved out.

2

Open the cavity

Wet drywall is cut back to dry edges, and wet insulation is removed.

3

Dry the framing

Air movers and a dehumidifier dry the joists and the space above.

4

Repair

New drywall, texture and paint follow once the framing reads dry.

Skipping the drying step and painting over a stain is how a ceiling leak returns, usually with a mold smell.

Textured ceilings and matching the finish

Popcorn and swirl textures are hard to match with a patch. Crews often feather the new texture into the old one and repaint the whole ceiling for a uniform look. Older textured ceilings may contain materials that need testing before they are disturbed, so mention the age of the home when you call.
