Norton Water Damage Pros is a dispatch line for water damage restoration in Norton, Massachusetts. We answer calls around the clock, collect the details of the loss and send a licensed and insured restoration crew. The business address is 246 E Main St, Norton, MA 02766.

What the line does

When you call, a dispatcher asks about the source of the water, how deep it is, whether anyone is hurt and whether electrical equipment is wet. The answers shape the crew and the equipment that are sent. The dispatcher also gives you safe first steps while you wait, such as closing a valve or moving valuables.

The line does not replace your insurer or a plumber. It connects you to restoration professionals who remove water, dry the structure and document the work. For the cause of a leak, you may need a plumber, a roofer or an HVAC technician as well.

How we describe our work

Every page on this site is written to explain what happens, what affects the price and what to do next. We avoid promises we cannot keep. We do not guarantee arrival times or insurance approval, and we do not quote prices without seeing the loss.

We point to published standards instead of making claims about ourselves. ANSI/IICRC S500 describes water damage restoration and S520 describes mold remediation, and we encourage homeowners to ask any company whether its crews work to them.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher records your address, the source of the water, the depth and any hazards, and confirms a callback number. A crew is sent when one is available, and the dispatcher tells you what to do in the meantime. On arrival, the crew checks safety, stops or confirms the source, removes standing water and maps moisture with meters and a thermal camera.

Before work begins, the scope and price are explained to you. The crew then extracts water, removes materials that cannot be dried, places drying equipment and returns to take readings until the structure matches a dry reference. You receive photos and readings you can pass to your insurer.

What we do not do

We do not give legal, insurance or medical advice. We do not promise that a claim will be approved, and we do not set the amount an insurer pays. We do not guarantee arrival times, because weather and demand change them. We do not diagnose health conditions, and questions about mold exposure belong with a physician.

We also do not hide behind jargon. If a step is needed, the crew explains why. If a step is optional, they say so.

Standards we point to

Two published standards guide the industry. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, including water categories and drying goals. ANSI/IICRC S520 covers mold remediation. Homeowners can ask any company whether its crews work to these standards, and what documents they provide.

The EPA and the Massachusetts Division of Insurance publish consumer guidance on moisture control and insurance. We link to what they say on our pages and encourage you to read it directly.

Why the line works this way

Water damage is time-sensitive, and most callers do not know which kind of company to call. A single line lets you describe the problem once. The dispatcher decides what kind of crew is needed, whether the loss needs pumps, drying, containment or all three, and sends the right people. That saves you from phoning several companies while water spreads.

The same line also handles calls that are not emergencies. If you want to ask about a stain, a smell or a plan for drying, call and ask. A short conversation can tell you whether a visit is needed, and there is no obligation to book anything after you ask your question. Callers can also ask which first steps are safe to take on their own.

How to reach us

Call (508) 286-8971 any time. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day. You can also write to contact@nortonwaterdamagepros.com, though a call is faster in an emergency. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Our crews follow ANSI/IICRC S500 where it applies.
