South Easton, MA is served by our 24/7 emergency line for water damage restoration, and ZIP code 02375 is the one the post office uses for the village. It lies along Route 138, which runs through the south of Easton.

Call any time of day or night. Tell the dispatcher your street, what is wet and whether the water has stopped. If it has not, close the nearest valve before you pick up the phone.

Emergency water removal in South Easton

Emergency water removal in South Easton follows the same sequence as elsewhere: stop the source, remove standing water, open what has to open and dry what has to dry. The difference is the route. South Easton sits close to Norton, so the crew's travel is short.

Calls from the village most often involve a basement that filled after rain, a failed appliance line or a burst pipe in cold weather. The dispatcher asks about each one in turn.

Route 138, brooks and wetlands around South Easton

Easton's planning report on Mass.gov lists a culvert under Route 138 near a mobile home park that has no existing drainage. Route 138 runs through South Easton, and a road that sheds water into a ditch with no outlet is the kind of detail that explains a flooded yard.

The same report names Mulberry Brook, which feeds a town well in Furnace Village, and the Canoe River, which is an aquifer and area of critical environmental concern. It also notes that the area around 305 Turnpike Street sits near the Hockomock Swamp, with a high water table and stormwater problems. These are Easton-wide conditions, so check the FEMA flood map for your address.

If Water Is Still Spreading in South Easton

Do these things while the crew is on the way.

Valve first

Find the main shutoff, usually where the line enters the basement, and close it.

Power check

If the panel is dry, shut off power to wet rooms. If not, stay out.

Lift and block

Raise furniture legs onto foil or blocks and lift rugs.

Note the time

Write down when you found the water and what you did, then photograph it.

The dispatcher can repeat these steps on the phone.

Open at any hour, including when the roads are wet

South Easton's emergency line is available 24 hours a day, every day. Storms bring the most calls, and the line stays open whether it is quiet or busy. If many homes are affected, the dispatcher still takes your information and tells you what to do while you wait.

Same-day response is offered when a crew is available. During a widespread storm, tell the dispatcher about any hazard such as a downed line or water near the panel, and that call is handled first.

Older village housing and what water does to it

South Easton has a mix of older homes near the village center and newer homes in surrounding streets. Older houses often have basements with stone or block walls and wooden framing close to grade. Moisture reaches those materials from the ground as well as from above.

Wet wood framing dries slowly, and trapped moisture can cause mold if it is not dried. The steps for protecting walls are on the wall and drywall repair page.

Questions to ask before you hire anyone

Ask whether the company works to ANSI/IICRC S500, how it documents moisture readings, how it handles contaminated water, and what the scope and price include before work starts. Those questions apply to any water damage restoration company in South Easton, not only ours.
