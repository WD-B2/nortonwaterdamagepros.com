A frozen pipe often looks fine until it thaws. The ice blockage splits the pipe or a fitting, and the water shows up as the pipe warms, sometimes hours after you noticed no flow. Emergency frozen pipe water damage calls in Norton go to our 24/7 line, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available.

If a faucet runs dry in cold weather, shut off the main valve before anything thaws. Open the faucet to relieve pressure, keep heat on in the house, and do not use a flame or torch on a pipe. If water is already coming through a wall or ceiling, call right away.

When a thaw turns into an emergency

The emergency starts when the pipe splits. Water pushes out of a crack as the ice melts, and a wall cavity can fill for a long time before a stain shows on the other side. A warm spell after a hard freeze is when many of these calls come in.

Check the National Weather Service forecast for freeze warnings, which lets you open cabinet doors and let a faucet drip before the cold arrives. Pipes in exterior walls, in unheated garages and in crawl spaces are the usual victims, and each is worth checking after the thaw begins.

Why the damage shows up late

Ice holds the water back, so the leak starts only when the temperature rises. That timing fools homeowners into thinking the pipe survived. The first sign is often a wet floor, a sagging ceiling or the sound of running water inside a wall.

The practical step is to shut off the main valve as soon as you suspect a frozen line and to leave it off until a plumber has looked at the section that froze. Restoration then focuses on drying the area the water reached, which is covered in wall and drywall water damage repair.

Before the thaw: a short checklist

These steps cost little and often prevent the call. They help most in the first hard cold of the season.

Cold-weather checklist for a Norton home

  • Know where the main shutoff is and make sure the handle turns
  • Open cabinet doors on exterior walls so room heat reaches the pipes
  • Let one faucet drip on the coldest nights, on the far side of the house from the meter
  • Disconnect and drain garden hoses and shut the outdoor spigot's inside valve
  • Keep the thermostat on in vacant rooms and any space with plumbing

None of this guarantees a pipe will not freeze, and an older home with pipes in an exterior wall is the hardest case. If a pipe does burst, the burst pipe cleanup page covers the next steps.

What affects the cost after a frozen pipe leak

A pipe that split in one wall differs from one that split above a finished ceiling.

Location of the split

Behind plaster or tile means more demolition to reach it.

Run time

The longer the line leaked after the thaw, the more material is soaked.

Area wet

Floors, ceilings and walls below the split add scope.

Drying days

Cold, damp weather can slow drying, so equipment may run longer.

Repairs after

Replacement drywall, paint and trim come after the structure is dry.
