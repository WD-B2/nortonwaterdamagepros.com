Plainville, MA has one ZIP code, 02762, and our emergency line covers it day and night. The town sits in Norfolk County, not Bristol County, which matters for permits and records. Ask the Plainville building department about local rules rather than assuming Norton's apply.

If you are calling about water that is still coming in, close the nearest valve or shut off the supply at the main. Then call and tell the dispatcher what you see.

Water damage in Plainville: what the town's report says

Plainville's Community Resilience Building report on Mass.gov lists wind, severe storms and flood or drought cycles as top hazards. It names flooding or sewer overflow during heavy rain at Shepard Street, West Bacon Street, Lakeside Drive, Branch Avenue and South Street, and notes the senior center parking lot often floods.

The same report says Town Center has infiltration into old clay sewer pipes. That matters for homes and businesses in the center, because an overloaded sewer line is a route for backups. See sewage backup cleanup for what that cleanup involves.

Lakes, ponds and watersheds around town

Plainville straddles the Blackstone, Ten Mile and Taunton River watersheds. Its lakes and ponds include Turnpike Lake, Lake Mirimichi, Fuller Pond and Wetherells Pond, and dams are listed at Shepard Street, Wetherells Pond and Lake Mirimichi.

Homes near water see higher groundwater in wet seasons, and a basement near a pond is more likely to take on water. Pumping and dehumidifying are the usual response, as explained under basement water damage cleanup.
