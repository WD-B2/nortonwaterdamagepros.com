The fastest way to reach us is by phone. Call (508) 286-8971, any hour, and a dispatcher will answer. For non-urgent questions you can email contact@nortonwaterdamagepros.com.

Call for emergencies

If water is entering the house, a pipe has burst or a basement is flooding, call right away. Say what you see, how deep it is and whether the source is stopped. The dispatcher will give you safe steps and arrange a crew. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Our crews follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard where it applies.

Before you call

Have these details ready if you can: your street address, what is wet, approximate depth, whether the water is still entering and whether electrical outlets are affected. If you can, photograph the damage first. None of this is required, and the dispatcher will ask.
